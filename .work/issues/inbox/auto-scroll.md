title: Auto-scroll
status: needs-triage
---

Move through a Song on its own while playing, hands-free. Postponed, not rejected, in the v1 spec's Out of Scope (`.work/prds/lyrics-display-v1.md`).

It goes against how the app shows a Song now: the Fit lays the Lyrics out as whole Screens so nothing moves while playing, and a page-turner pedal turns them. Triage should settle what Auto-scroll would mean here:

- Scrolling one long column, which gives up the Fit's columns; or
- Turning Screens on a timer, which cannot follow the player's tempo the way the pedal does.

Worth revisiting after Setlists, which may cover most of the hands-free need.
