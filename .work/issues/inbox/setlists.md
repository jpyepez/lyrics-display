Ordered groups of Songs from the Library, for playing a set. Postponed, not rejected, in the v1 spec's Out of Scope (`.work/prds/lyrics-display-v1.md`).

Why it may be worth doing: the pedal already turns Screens within a Song. With a Setlist it could go on from the last Screen of one Song to the first Screen of the next, so a whole set plays without touching the device.

Open questions for triage:

- Can a Song be in more than one Setlist? Does deleting a Song take it out of its Setlists?
- Does the pedal carry on from one Song into the next, and how does the Screen show which Song is next?
- Is a Setlist printed as one stack of Sheets, one Song per Sheet?
- Do Setlists go into the Library file for export/import?
